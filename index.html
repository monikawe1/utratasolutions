Communication compliance

Safe, wanted and identifiable communication.

Permitted communication use

Utrata Solutions may contact clients, business partners or people who have directly contacted us for legitimate purposes such as:

  • responding to a direct enquiry;
  • project coordination and operational updates;
  • appointment or meeting reminders;
  • customer support and service administration;
  • transactional information related to an agreed service.

Prohibited communication use

We do not use communication services to:

  • send unsolicited bulk messages or spam;
  • purchase, rent or scrape contact databases;
  • send phishing, deceptive or fraudulent content;
  • hide or misrepresent the sender's identity;
  • continue messaging after a valid opt-out request;
  • sell or transfer messaging consent to another party.

Consent, identification and opt-out

Consent: Before sending ongoing SMS or similar application-to-person messages, we obtain the recipient's prior informed consent for the relevant sender and message purpose. Where written consent is legally or contractually required, we obtain and retain it.

Sender identification: Messages identify Utrata Solutions as the sender unless they are part of an active conversation in which our identity is already clear.

Opt-out: Initial ongoing SMS messages include “Reply STOP to unsubscribe” or an equivalent standard instruction. A recipient may opt out at any time in one step. After an opt-out, we may send one confirmation and then stop further messages unless the person provides new consent.

Help: Recipients may reply HELP where supported or email contact@utratasolutions.com.
